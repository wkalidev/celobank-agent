PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2025-06-20

1. OVERVIEW

CeloBank Agent is a non-custodial DeFi interface. We are committed to minimising data collection. We do not sell your data.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

  • Chat messages — sent to our AI backend (Claude/Anthropic) to generate responses. Not stored persistently after processing.
  • Wallet address — read from your connected wallet to query on-chain balances and prepare transactions. Never stored server-side.
  • Usage analytics — anonymous page-view data via Vercel's default infrastructure analytics.

3. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

  • Private keys or seed phrases (impossible — App is non-custodial)
  • Phone numbers or personal identity information
  • Payment card or bank details

4. LOCAL STORAGE

The App stores daily-streak data in your browser's localStorage keyed by your wallet address. This data never leaves your device.

5. THIRD PARTIES

  • Anthropic — AI inference provider. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • CoinGecko — price data API (no personal data sent).
  • Celo RPC (forno.celo.org) — public blockchain node (no personal data sent).

6. BLOCKCHAIN DATA

Transactions you sign are broadcast to the public Celo blockchain. All on-chain data is permanently public and irreversible by design.

7. CHILDREN

The App is not directed at users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. CHANGES

We may update this Policy at any time. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

9. CONTACT
